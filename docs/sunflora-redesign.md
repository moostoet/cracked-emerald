# Sunflora redesign

Sunflora is a **Grass/Fire special attacker and sun supporter**. Its three abilities give it a choice between speed, power, and setting weather for its team.

## Typing and stats

| Stat | Before | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| HP | 75 | 85 |
| Attack | 75 | 55 |
| Defense | 55 | 75 |
| Special Attack | 105 | 125 |
| Special Defense | 85 | 85 |
| Speed | 30 | 55 |
| Total | 425 | 480 |

The extra bulk helps Sunflora survive long enough to attack or support its team. Base 55 Speed leaves it reliant on Chlorophyll, positioning, or Trick Room against faster opponents. Grass/Fire is weak to Flying, Poison, and Rock; Stealth Rock and weather changes remain useful counterplay.

Sunkern stays pure Grass and still evolves with a Sun Stone.

## Abilities

- **Chlorophyll:** doubles Speed in sun for a setup attacker.
- **Solar Power:** boosts special damage in sun at the cost of HP each turn. Giga Drain and Morning Sun help manage that cost.
- **Drought (hidden):** replaces Early Bird and sets sun on entry, supporting Sunflora and its teammates. Heat Rock extends the weather.

These are alternative abilities. Sunflora does not receive Drought and Solar Power or Chlorophyll at the same time.

## Moves

The active Generation 9 level-up list gives Ember on evolution and through the move reminder, Weather Ball through the move reminder, Incinerate at 16, Sunny Day at 19, Giga Drain at 22, Morning Sun at 25, Solar Beam at 31, Flamethrower at 34, Earth Power at 39, Leaf Storm at 43, and Fire Blast at 46.

Fire Blast, Flamethrower, Heat Wave, Incinerate, and Overheat are added to its move compatibility, alongside Ember. Which of these are teachable depends on the hack's available TMs and tutors. The website generates this list using the same helpers as the ROM. Earlier-generation optional level-up configurations retain their historical lists.
