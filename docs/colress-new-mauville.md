# Colress in New Mauville

Colress, the scientist from Unova, is studying the power inside New Mauville. He stands on the generator's shut-off switch, so the player has to battle him before turning the generator off. After losing, he gives the player the **Mega Ring**, which lets the player's Pokémon Mega Evolve.

## Reaching the fight

New Mauville opens after the fifth badge. Wattson appears in Mauville City after Norman is beaten and hands over the Basement Key, and the entrance on Route 110 is reached by Surf. Colress waits in the generator room at the back of New Mauville.

He tells the player that New Mauville is teeming with a natural power that its founders saw potential in and tried to harness, and he wants to see whether a Pokémon's true strength comes from a power like that or from its Trainer.

## Level cap

The fight is a hard level-cap checkpoint. After the third badge, the cap is **41** until Colress is beaten, and then **42** until the sixth badge. The visit is optional, so if the player earns the sixth badge without beating Colress, his cap stops applying and the usual caps continue.

## The battle

Every battle against Colress starts on permanent **Electric Terrain**. Grounded Pokémon's Electric moves are 1.3 times as strong, and grounded Pokémon can't fall asleep. The terrain helps the player's grounded Electric moves too. Defog removes it, and other terrain moves replace it, but Colress's Magnezone can bring it back.

Three of his Pokémon, Probopass, Magnezone, and Vikavolt, evolve only inside New Mauville in this hack, so the generator's power literally built his machines. His ace Mega Evolves on its first turn, right before he gives up the Mega Ring.

Probopass survives one hit with Sturdy and sets Stealth Rock. Magnezone puts up Reflect for the team's Ground-weak members and restores Electric Terrain if the player changes it. Porygon-Z uses Conversion to turn itself Electric-type, so its Thunderbolt gets Adaptability on top of the terrain. Golurk, the only physical attacker, gets around special-attack drops such as Serperior's Noble Aura. Levitate Vikavolt is immune to Ground moves and checks Grass types with Bug Buzz. Mega Ampharos warns of a doubled Thunderbolt with Charge, and Mold Breaker lets its Electric moves hit through Volt Absorb and Lightning Rod.

Colress uses Smart Trainer AI and keeps Ampharos as his last Pokémon. All of these moves are in the Pokémon's learnsets. Golurk is three levels below Golett's evolution level.

## The Mega Ring

After the battle, Colress explains that Mega Evolution unlocks a power hidden within Pokémon when a Trainer and Pokémon share a strong bond, then gives the player the Mega Ring and leaves. With the Mega Ring in the bag, a Pokémon holding its Mega Stone can Mega Evolve in battle. If the player's Key Items pocket is somehow full, Colress waits and gives the ring the next time they talk to him.

This is a design implemented in the ROM data. Build and test checks verify the implementation; the fight's difficulty still needs gameplay testing.
